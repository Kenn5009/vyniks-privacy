Vyniks Connect Account & Data Deletion

Vyniks Connect respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to delete their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Vyniks Connect account directly from within the app.

  1. Open the Vyniks Connect app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the instructions displayed by the app to confirm the deletion.
Important: Account deletion is intended to be permanent. Please make sure you want to delete your account before confirming the request.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When you confirm account deletion, Vyniks Connect will process the deletion of your account and associated user data according to the application's data-management procedures.

This may include account information, profile information, user-generated content and other data associated with your Vyniks Connect account, where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

Where information is retained for these purposes, it will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and deleted when it is no longer required.

Need Help?

If you are unable to access the Delete Account option or need assistance with an account deletion request, please contact Vyniks Connect support through the contact information provided in the app or on the Vyniks Connect Privacy Policy page.
