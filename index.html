Vyniks Connect Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Vyniks Connect ("Vyniks", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Vyniks Connect mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Vyniks Connect, we may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Username, display name, profile information, and profile photographs.
  • Posts, comments, likes, followers, and other content you choose to publish.
  • Photos, videos, stories, reels, and other media that you upload.
  • Messages and other communications you send through the application.
  • Information you provide when contacting us for support.

We may also automatically receive certain technical information, including device information, application information, IP address, diagnostic information, and notification-related identifiers.

2. Push Notifications

Vyniks Connect may use Firebase Cloud Messaging or similar notification services to deliver notifications to your device.

These notifications may include information about messages, interactions, followers, comments, likes, and other activity within the application.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Provide and operate Vyniks Connect.
  • Display your profile and content to other users according to the application's features and settings.
  • Deliver messages and notifications.
  • Provide social features such as following, likes, comments, and interactions.
  • Improve application performance, security, and reliability.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, and violations of our policies.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. User-Generated Content

Vyniks Connect allows users to create and share content. Content that you choose to make public may be visible to other users.

You should not upload or share information that you do not want other users to see.

5. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate parts of Vyniks Connect, including authentication, database, storage, analytics, notifications, hosting, communications, and other technical services.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When you delete your account, information associated with your account may be deleted or anonymized, subject to technical requirements and applicable legal obligations.

8. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Vyniks Connect account and associated personal information through account-management features available in the application or by contacting us.

Some information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate operational purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Vyniks Connect is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in Vyniks Connect, you may be able to:

  • Update your profile information.
  • Control certain notification settings.
  • Delete content you have posted.
  • Manage privacy-related settings.
  • Request deletion of your account.

You may also control certain device permissions through your device settings.

11. Third-Party Services

Vyniks Connect may integrate third-party services, including services used for authentication, cloud storage, databases, notifications, communications, and application functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we may update the effective date displayed at the beginning of this policy. Continued use of Vyniks Connect after an update means that you acknowledge the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the Vyniks Connect support team through the support/contact options provided within the application or through the contact information published with the application.
